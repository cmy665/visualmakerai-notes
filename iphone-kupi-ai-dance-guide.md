# The iPhone Kupi Dance Trend: How to Star in It With One Photo

The iPhone Kupi trend pairs playful moves with the "iPhone Kupi" sound. You know it when you see it: a phone unfolds into an oversized screen, then comes the step-back dance and finally the overhead toss. Joining in normally means learning the routine and filming several takes. A photo-based AI edit skips the rehearsal. Here's how it works and how to get a good first take.

## What the edit does

On the [iPhone Kupi AI dance page from Visual Maker AI](https://visualmakerai.com/ai-video-effects/iphone-kupi-ai), you pick an original routine and replace its lead with your photo. The original video guides the moves, the phone prop, the setting and the timing across all 15 seconds. Your photo only decides who's dancing.

There are three routines to choose from:

- **Studio:** a white suit, a playful reveal and a room full of retro rhythm.
- **Glam:** bold styling and big main-character energy.
- **Pet:** a cat takes its turn in the spotlight, built for animals.

Studio and Glam are for adults; Pet is for your animal.

## Step 1: Choose a photo that helps the AI

The page's photo tips are specific and worth following:

- A sharp, evenly lit photo with one face clearly visible.
- A head-and-shoulders or full-body shot.
- No sunglasses, filters or anything covering the face.
- For pets, show the face, fur markings and paws clearly.

Only upload yourself, your pet or an adult who has agreed.

## Step 2: Pick resolution

Select 720P or 1080P and confirm permission. Each 15-second edit costs one Video Pass at 720P or three at 1080P. The exact cost appears before generation, and passes for failed generations are returned. Video Passes are a one-time purchase with no subscription, and packs start at $8.99 for one pass.

## Step 3: Add the sound when you post

The download is a silent 9:16 vertical MP4. That's deliberate. You add the iPhone Kupi sound from TikTok's sound library when you post. Sound availability varies by platform, region and account, so check that the sound is available to you before planning a campaign around it.

## Checking your result

AI movement can be imperfect. Before posting, look at:

1. **Hands and feet** during the step-back, where errors show up most.
2. **Identity** in the overhead toss, when the face is moving fastest.
3. **The phone prop** unfolding cleanly.

If something drifts, a sharper, better-lit photo usually fixes more than switching routines does.

## Ideas for the clip

- Pick the Pet routine for the most replayable version.
- Make a matching pair: you in Studio, your pet in Pet, posted back to back.
- Use Glam for a birthday or "new phone, who dis" announcement.

The routine is already there. All you add is a good photo and the right sound.
