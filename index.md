# Making a Hotel Lobby AI Duo Video From Two Photos

The orange backdrop, the single microphone and two performers trading lines are a look many people recognize from Quavo and Takeoff's "HOTEL LOBBY." The Hotel Lobby AI trend borrows that staging: creators place friends, couples or even a person and their pet into the same orange studio for a short duet. This guide covers how to prepare your photos and choose settings.

## Step 1: Upload two photos

You need one image per performer. Good pairs share a few traits:

- **Clear and well-lit.** Front-facing portraits help keep recognizable details.
- **Similar quality.** If one photo is sharp and the other blurry, the duo will look uneven.
- **Permission.** Both people, or the pet's owner, should be happy to appear.

The examples on the page show three common setups: two friends sharing the mic, a couple taking turns, and a corgi joining its owner.

## Step 3: Generate, add music, share

After generating, review the 8-second result and download or share it. Add music you're allowed to use before publishing. That matters because the trend is tied to a commercial song.

## Tips for a better duet

1. **Match the energy.** Two smiling portraits read better together than one smiling and one serious.
2. **Mind the order.** Decide who should be the main performer and keep your uploads consistent across retries.
3. **Pets work best facing forward.** A side-on dog photo gives the model little to animate.
4. **Keep the caption short.** Eight seconds goes fast, and a one-line caption ("Us at every party") does more than a paragraph.

## Know the limits

The page notes that its concept preview is an original illustration and that results vary with the photos you upload. Treat the examples as a guide to what's possible, not a guarantee.

## Why people like it

The format is simple and instantly recognizable, and it works for almost any pair: best friends, siblings, a couple's anniversary, or you and your cat. Two good photos are all you need to start.
