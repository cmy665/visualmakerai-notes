Reading Visual Maker AI's Model Price Table Before You Generate

The Models page on Visual Maker AI is a price list, not a marketing brochure. It names the image and video models available in the generator, who provides them, and how many credits each option deducts from your account. The page is dated 2026-10-20, and it warns that credit costs can change when model pricing changes. The quote in the generator is the amount charged.

That last sentence is the practical rule. A listed price is the base charge. A model can cost more, and it is never charged below these figures. The generator shows the exact total for your settings before you submit.

Image models: credits per still, by resolution

Image models are billed per image. Resolution changes the cost. Quality controls do not change the credit cost. The supported aspect ratios, for these image models, are 1:1, 2:3, 3:2, 3:4, 4:3, 9:16, and 16:9.

Google's Nano Banana 2 is 10 credits at 1K, 16 at 2K, and 24 at 4K, with no quality selector. Nano Banana 2.1 is 8, 12, and 18 at those resolutions, accepts up to 10 reference images, and also has no quality selector. Nano Banana Pro is 16 credits at 1K or 2K, and 28 at 4K.

OpenAI's GPT Image 2 is 6, 10, and 16 credits at 1K, 2K, and 4K. Quality controls do not change that cost. GPT Image 2.5 Flare and GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst both cost 12, 20, and 32, and Flare accepts up to 10 reference images.

ByteDance's Seedream 5.0 Pro is 14 credits at 1K and 28 at 2K, with no 4K option. It accepts up to 10 reference images. The first reference is included; each additional reference adds 1 credit.

If you are choosing only on cost, the page's own guidance is direct. The lowest image cost is GPT Image 2 at 1K, at 6 credits. Nano Banana 2.1 at 1K is 8 credits. For fast Google stills, use Nano Banana 2; for more detailed Google stills, use Nano Banana Pro. For reference editing, Seedream 5.0 Pro, Nano Banana 2.1, and GPT Image 2.5 accept up to 10 reference images.

Video models: per clip or per second

Veo 3.1 is priced per clip. Every other video model is priced per second. When a per-second rate is not a whole number, the total is rounded up.

Veo 3.1 charges the same for 4, 6, and 8 seconds. Reference-to-video is Lite or Fast, and 8 seconds only. At 720p, 1080p, and 4K, Lite is 30 / 45 / 150 credits, Fast is 60 / 75 / 180, and Quality is 450 / 465 / 570.

Seedance 2 is billed per second for 4-15 seconds, at 82 credits per second in 720p, 204 in 1080p, and 416 in 4K. Seedance 2.5 is 56 credits per second at 480p and 126 at 720p, for 3-30 seconds. Seedance 2.5 jobs that include a source video are quoted from the output length plus the source clip length.

Kling 3.0 is 28 credits per second at 720p and 36 at 1080p, for 3-15 seconds, without audio. MiniMax H3 is 16 credits per second at 768P and 26 at 2K, for 4-15 seconds, and each input image, including each first or last frame, adds 8 credits.

Wan 3.0 Video is 16 / 36 / 64 credits per second at 480p, 720p, and 1080p. Wan 3.0 Video Prime is about 24.5 / 50.4 / 100.8 at those resolutions, so an 8-second clip is 196 / 404 / 808. Vidu Q4 includes audio and is billed per second for 3-16 seconds, with image-to-video or reference-to-video of up to 15 images; text-only and first/last-frame modes are unavailable.

The page works through a few examples so the arithmetic is not left as an exercise: Seedance 2 at 720p for 8 seconds is 656 credits; Kling 3.0 at 1080p for 5 seconds is 180; Wan 3.0 Video at 720p for 8 seconds is 288; Veo 3.1 Quality at 4K is 570 credits for the whole clip.

The "how to choose" section also offers a short-video hint: Kling 3.0 is 28 credits per second at 720p without audio, while Wan 3.0 Video is 16 credits per second at 480p. If you want one price for the whole clip, Veo 3.1 Lite at 720p is 30 credits. For image-led video with audio, a 5-second Vidu Q4 clip at 540p is 72 credits.

None of this replaces the quote you will see in the text-to-image or video generator before you confirm. The table tells you the floor, the billing unit, and which models accept reference images. The generator tells you what this run will actually cost.
