# Three Photography Prompts, Dissected: Editorial Portrait, Product on White, Street Lifestyle

A good photography prompt reads like a photographer's shot list: subject, light, lens, mood. The [Photography & Portraits prompt category](https://visualmakerai.com/prompt-library/photography) in the Visual Maker AI library is small, but its three copy-ready prompts show those habits clearly. Here is what each one is doing and how to adapt it.

## Prompt 1: the editorial portrait

The prompt asks for an editorial portrait of a woman with natural window light, shallow depth of field, an 85mm lens, film grain and muted tones.

Why it works: every phrase narrows the look. The light source is named (window light), the focus behavior is named (shallow depth of field), the lens is a classic portrait focal length, and the finish is specified (film grain, muted tones). To adapt it, change only the subject and keep the technical phrases. A portrait of an older man or a child's drawing-table scene would still inherit the same soft, restrained look.

## Prompt 2: product on white

This one asks for a studio product photo of wireless earbuds on a pure white background, with soft box lighting, sharp details and an e-commerce catalog style.

Why it works: it names the setting (studio, white background), the lighting tool (soft box) and the destination (e-commerce catalog), which tells the model what a successful image looks like. To adapt it, swap earbuds for your product and keep the rest. If you are shooting several products for a single shop, reusing the same phrases helps the set look consistent.

## Prompt 3: street lifestyle

The third prompt describes a candid street lifestyle shot at golden hour with an urban backdrop, a 35mm lens and an authentic film look.

Why it works: "candid" and "authentic film look" set the tone, golden hour sets the light, and 35mm suggests a wider, more documentary frame than the 85mm portrait. Treat it as a skeleton: add who is in the frame and what they are doing, and drop any wording that does not match your scene.

## A reusable pattern

Put side by side, the three prompts share the same skeleton:

- **Subject:** one clear noun phrase.
- **Light:** window light, soft box, or golden hour.
- **Lens or framing:** 85mm, catalog framing, 35mm.
- **Finish:** grain, muted tones, sharp details, film look.

When your own prompt feels vague, check whether one of these four slots is missing.

## Practical tips

- Change one slot at a time so you can see what each phrase does.
- Try the same subject with two lenses, such as 35mm and 85mm, to compare framing.
- Set the aspect ratio for the destination first: portrait for a feed, wide for a banner.
